Triage: Pipelane triages inbound leads automatically and scores each one against the sales team's own qualification rules. Every lead in the queue shows the reason it was ranked.

Routing: Pipelane routes each qualified lead to a rep by territory and segment rules. Reps are notified in Slack and by email as soon as a lead is assigned.

CRM sync: Pipelane syncs two ways with Salesforce and HubSpot. An import tool maps fields from the old CRM, which helps teams that are migrating CRMs.

Onboarding: Connecting a CRM takes one guided onboarding session. Pipelane does not require a services engagement to go live.

Prioritised queue: New SDRs get a prioritised queue, so they work the best leads first. Managers set the rules and SDRs do not need to learn a scoring model.

Analytics: Dashboards show lead response time and conversion by source. Managers can compare reps and territories.

Security: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. A SOC 2 Type II report is available on request. EU data hosting is available.

Pricing: The Team plan costs $49 per seat per month, billed annually. The Growth plan costs $79 per seat per month, billed annually. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial.

Support: Email support is available on weekdays. The Growth plan includes a dedicated success manager.

Limits: Pipelane is cloud-hosted only. Pipelane does not send email on a rep's behalf.
